Common questions

My child's therapist gave us a word list. Can I type it in?

Yes. That is the whole point. Words → Add, then pick the sound each word targets. You can also start from the built-in lists for a sound and edit them.

Does the app listen to my child?

No. There is no microphone, no speech recognition, and no scoring algorithm. The adult in the room taps whether the attempt was clear, close, or missed. Software still cannot judge articulation reliably, and an app that guesses wrong teaches the wrong thing.

What does the free version do?

A set number of practice sessions with everything unlocked. After that, a one-time purchase removes the limit and turns on the PDF report. No subscription, nothing recurring.

I paid on another device. How do I get it back?

Settings → Restore Purchases. It uses the same Apple ID that bought it.

Can I send the report to the therapist?

Report → Share. It makes a one-page PDF laid out to hand over at the next session: which words were practiced, on which days, and how each attempt went.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Delete Everything. It erases the stored file immediately. Deleting the app does the same. Nothing was ever sent anywhere, so there is nothing to delete on a server.

Is this a medical device?

No. It is a practice tool for doing the homework a therapist already assigned. It does not diagnose, treat, or replace a speech-language pathologist.
