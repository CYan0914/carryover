Privacy Policy

Carryover: Speech Practice · last updated 5 November 2026

The short version: everything you record stays on your phone. There is no account, no server, and nothing is sent to us.

What we collect

Nothing. The app has no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising SDK, and no third-party libraries that transmit data.

What the app stores, and where

The app keeps a single file on your device, inside the app's own storage. It contains:

  • the child's first name, if you choose to enter one (it is optional)
  • the words you added, and which sound each word targets
  • the practice record: when you practiced, which words, and how you judged each attempt

That file never leaves the device unless you export it yourself.

Microphone and audio

The app never records audio and does not listen. It speaks words aloud using the system voice that is built into iOS, and it asks for no microphone permission. Attempts are judged by the adult in the room, not by software.

Sharing

The only way data leaves the app is when you tap Share to send the one-page PDF or the JSON backup. That uses the standard iOS share sheet, and you choose the destination. We are not involved and receive nothing.

Purchases

The one-time unlock is processed by Apple. We receive no payment details and no personal information from Apple. Apple's handling of that transaction is covered by Apple's own privacy policy.

Children

The app is intended for use by an adult, on behalf of a child. It collects no data from anyone, so it collects nothing from children. The only child information stored is a first name you type in yourself, and you can leave it blank or delete it at any time in Settings.

Deleting your data

Settings has a Delete Everything button. It removes the stored file from the device immediately and permanently. Deleting the app does the same.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it. A change that affects what is collected would ship with an app update, not silently.
